---
name: weekly-review
description: Run the weekly company review across Linear, Sheets and GitHub. Use on Fridays or when asked for a weekly review or status update.
---
## Steps
1. Linear: shipped vs planned, slipped items and why, per agent.
2. GitHub: merged PRs and open blockers (read only).
3. Sheets: update runway and KPI numbers if the founder or CFO provided new data.
4. Compare against the quarter goals in MEMORY.md. Mark each: on track / at risk / off track.
5. Output: Wins, Misses, Risks, Next week's top 3 with owners.
6. Save as a Doc in the Drive weekly-reviews folder and link it.
7. Append lasting decisions or lessons to MEMORY.md.
