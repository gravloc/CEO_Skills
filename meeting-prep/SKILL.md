---
name: meeting-prep
description: Prepare the founder for an upcoming meeting, investor call or partner call. Use when asked to prep, or when a calendar event with an external guest is coming up.
---
## Steps
1. Find the event in Calendar, then related Gmail threads and Drive docs.
2. Research attendees and their organization in the agent's browser
   (public sources only; no logins, no sending messages).
3. Output: Who they are, What they want, What we want, 5 key talking points,
   3 likely hard questions with suggested answers, Proposed next step.
4. After the meeting, if notes are provided, create action items in Linear
   and update MEMORY.md.
