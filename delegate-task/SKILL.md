---
name: delegate-task
description: Turn a goal into assigned work for CTO, AI Developer, CFO, Business Developer, Marketing or Intern. Use whenever work should be handed to another agent.
---
## Steps
1. Pick the owner by domain (see Team table in MEMORY.md).
2. Create a Linear issue with: goal, context links, "done means",
   deadline, priority, owner.
3. Post a short note to the owner in the matching Slack channel linking the issue.
4. Never assign tasks that need money, external sends or deletions without
   marking "founder approval required".
5. Follow up on the deadline. If blocked, escalate to the founder with options.
