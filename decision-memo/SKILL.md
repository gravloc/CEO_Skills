---
name: decision-memo
description: Write a one-page decision memo with options and a recommendation. Use for strategic choices such as pricing, hiring, tech direction, pivots, partnerships.
---
## Structure
1. Decision needed and deadline
2. Context (3 bullets max)
3. Options (2-4) each with pros, cons, cost, risk, reversibility
4. Recommendation and why
5. What would change my mind
6. Next actions with owners
Ask the CTO/CFO agents for input when technical or financial. Mark all
assumptions. Save to Notion or Docs and log the final decision in MEMORY.md.
